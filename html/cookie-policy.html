Cookie Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains cookies and similar technologies on the NGXLab website. At present, this site is a static educational landing page and its own code does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and scripts that can remember information about a browser or activity between visits.

What this site uses

The current NGXLab landing page does not use cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising pixels, or embedded third-party media. We therefore do not show a cookie consent banner for this page.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, and access time, in server logs to deliver and protect the site. These logs are not cookies controlled by this page. Hosting arrangements and retention practices should be checked with the provider.

Links to other websites

Links from NGXLab may take you to websites operated by other organisations. Their cookie and privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review. NGXLab does not control third-party websites.

Your browser settings

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Because the current landing page does not set cookies, changing those settings is not needed to use it. Browser controls do not affect technical logs maintained by a hosting provider.

Changes to this policy

If NGXLab adds cookies or similar technologies in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what they do and how they are used. Any consent or preference controls required for those technologies will be provided before they are used, where applicable.

Contact
