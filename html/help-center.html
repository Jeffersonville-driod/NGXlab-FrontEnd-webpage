HOW CAN WE HELP?

Help center

Answers about your practice account and the information shown in NGXLab.

Is my virtual balance real money?

No. It is simulated practice money with no cash value. You cannot deposit, withdraw, or transfer it.

Are practice orders actually placed?

No. The order form only previews an estimated whole-share quantity. It does not submit a trade or change your balance or portfolio.

Why might market prices not be live?

The market board displays the latest snapshot available from NGXLab’s backend. Check the trading-session date shown above the table; prices may be delayed.

Why does my portfolio show no holdings?

Portfolio holdings and trade history are not connected yet. They will appear once the backend supports simulated trade execution and portfolio tracking.

Where can I read the usage terms?

Review the NGXLab Terms & Conditions for information about this demo learning environment.
